### Freshman Year (RCDS)

English 9: Greek plays really brought mommy issues to the max

Honors Bio: GCAT or CGUA 🍉

Honors Algebra II: Ms. Khan is never letting another interviewing teacher teach us again

Honors Spanish 3: Guantanamera, Guajira Guantamera!

Honors French Intensive: Ohhh noooo, qu'est-ce que Rachid fait??

AP Comp Sci: Dude I spent all this time learning Java and I haven't used it since

World History 9: I remember more talk about Irish and meadows than about the Mongol conquests

### Sophomore/Lower Year

English 200: Mary Oliver might be a goated poet after all

History 201: So I think Christopher Columbus was not a great guy

Math 380 (Precalc): Those math majors were tough af

Chemistry 550 (AP Chem): That feeling when you need to derive the Henderson-Hasselbalch equation for a Spring 6-level question

Spanish 401: Debating Spain's economics was not on my bucket list for fall-term Andover

Phys Ed 200: There's nothing like helping the gym teacher (120lb MAX) belay a 6'4" 230lb baseball recruit

PHR 360 (Proof and Persuasion): I got all excited doing formal logic for the first week and then I had to write papers on epistemic bubbles :(

CSC 403 (Discrete Math): This is actually pretty cool, I sure hope no <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=47aSeHHnFAY" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">monstrosities</a> are made from this!

Music 410 (The Musical Brain): So this explains why I have to chant some stupid tiktok audio under my breath while playing hockey

Chinese 623 (Chinglish): I enjoyed doing the play but got a level so... you win some you lose some I guess?

Math 580 (Intro to Calc): We talked about limits for so long and I don't even think we did l'hopitals

Theatre 901 (A Production of <a href="https://www.playscripts.com/play/2822?srsltid=AfmBOopt5OuFrfHb-Sn4Izltl6mWzu186mcwxd7nt1DmSgSvpQRbPBHA" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Good Kids</a>): I am shocked at how few lines we screwed up during the actual performance given how terrible our dress runs were

Physics 551 (AP Physics C Mechanics Pt. 1): Thank goodness for the "drop your worst test" policy because test two I did NOT see the back page (not that I was going to finish it anyways)

### Junior/Upper Year

English 300: Never before have I thought so analytically about symbolism and gender roles and colonial power dynamics (yeah I read feminist literature, what about it?)

History 300: She Manifest on my Destiny til I engage in ethnocide through Native American boarding schools

Math 590 (Calc BC): Sometimes I'd solve the last challenge problem worth two points and I'd get the basic derivative questions wrong (worth 8 pts each) because my brain was in integral mode

Physics 552 (Physics C Mechanics Pt. 2): Why were our homework problems so much more challenging than test problems?? I swear I had to consult the answer key basically every homework assignment

Physics 553 (Physics C E&M Pt. 3): So my analogy for electrical force and gravitational force did not work 1:1 much to my embarrassment

Physics 554 (Physics C E&M Pt. 4): RC? LC? RL? What is going on? I'd rather derive your equations every test than have to memorize Q(t) = CE(1 - e<sup>-t/RC</sup>)

Chemistry 610/620 (Organic Chemistry): Unironically probably the most fun class I've been in -- after all, <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigmatropic_reaction" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">sigmas go where they're needed</a>

Math 535 (Stats Pt. 1): Correlation ≠ Causation. Nuff said

Chemistry 487 (Environmental Chemistry): If anything remotely watershed or environmentally related comes up in conversation I *will* be telling people to <a href="https://www3.erie.gov/environment/what-problem-car-washing target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">wash their cars on soil</a>

Physics 600 (Modern Physics): Gee, I sure hope Farmer John's 15 light year long barn does not have a ladder of 9 light years moving at 0.6c!
