This is day 4 of journaling with Selena. Today's prompt:

***Write about a small joy that never fails to make you happy. Why does it matter?***

Today I had an ice cream flavor called *Better than Sex*. It was a cake batter base swirled with heath bar and brownie chunks. It was so-so... I was expecting more given the name and the description. Either way, a nice treat after being baked out in the sun for four hours (my skin is already itching -- it's definitely sunburnt.)

I'm always one to try an exotic ice cream flavor, because unlike exotic foods, you really can't go wrong with ice cream. Like come on, you're gonna tell me a little bit of flavoring and mix-ins are going to screw up the holy duo of fat and sugar? 

I tried really hard to come up with some kind of metaphor for ice cream and how I live life or my values, but the best I could come up with is ice cream = people, as in I love meeting new people because in general they're all great and have slightly different combinations of things I'm generally familiar with.

And then now that I'm typing this out, it sounds pretty lame. Oh well. Just know that I really like ice cream.

Also shoutout to the people who are reading this and have reached out to me to let me know that they enjoy it, y'all have definitely brightened my day and given me even more motivation to journal. You are the cookie doughs and black raspberry swirls in my life.
